# Demo Guide

To demonstrate the full capability of the DealFlow AI platform:

1. **Start**: Navigate to the Landing Page. Observe the premium, cinematic design.
2. **Dashboard**: Click into the Workspace. Note the populated metrics and the initial demo opportunity.
3. **New Opportunity**: Click "New Opportunity" (`+`).
4. **Load Data**: Click "Load Demo Inquiry" to populate the form with a realistic unstructured cloud modernization request. Click "Analyze Opportunity".
5. **Agent Workflow**: In the Opportunity Workspace, click "Run Analysis Workflow".
6. **Observe Agents**: Watch the left sidebar as agents transition from 'Running' to 'Completed'.
7. **Review Results**: Click through the tabs:
   - **Requirements**: See structured technical/business requirements extracted from the inquiry.
   - **Capabilities**: View matched internal SMEs and offerings.
   - **Proposal**: See the AI-generated draft proposal. Note the "Requires Review" badge.
8. **Approve**: Click "Approve Proposal" on the Proposal tab.
9. **Follow-ups**: Check the generated Follow-up tasks assigned to Humans and AI.
10. **Activity Log**: Navigate to the global "Activity Log" to see the timeline of agent actions.
